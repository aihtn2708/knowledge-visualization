1. The Ecosystem: AI -> ML -> DL

Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Any technique enabling computers to mimic human behavior (Includes rigid Rule-based systems).

Machine Learning (ML)

Algorithms that learn patterns from data using statistical methods without explicit programming.

Deep Learning (DL)

A powerful subset of ML using multi-layered Artificial Neural Networks. Essential for Modern AI (Vision, LLMs).

AI Agents & Tool Calling (ReAct)

Agents loop through Reason -> Act -> Observe to achieve a goal autonomously.

Goal: "What is 15% of the Q3 Revenue ($50,000)?"
Thought: I need to calculate 15% of 50000. I am an AI and bad at math, I should use the calculator tool.
Action: call_calculator(expression="50000 * 0.15")
Observation: 7500
Thought: I now know the answer.
Final Answer: 15% of the Q3 revenue is $7,500.
