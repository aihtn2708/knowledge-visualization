RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

Injecting private knowledge into the prompt before the AI answers.

1. User Query

"What is our company's refund policy for software products?"

2. Semantic Search

Doc_301: "Software refunds are valid within 30 days of purchase..."
Doc_102: "Employee vacation days policy..."

3. Augmented Prompt

System: Answer using only this context:
"Software refunds are valid within 30 days..."

User: "What is the policy for software?"
AI: You can get a refund within 30 days.

AI Agents & Tool Calling (ReAct)

Agents loop through Reason -> Act -> Observe to achieve a goal autonomously.

Goal: "What is 15% of the Q3 Revenue ($50,000)?"
Thought: I need to calculate 15% of 50000. I am an AI and bad at math, I should use the calculator tool.
Action: call_calculator(expression="50000 * 0.15")
Observation: 7500
Thought: I now know the answer.
Final Answer: 15% of the Q3 revenue is $7,500.
