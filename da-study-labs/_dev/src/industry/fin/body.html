Investment Risk and Return Lab

A company's investment committee must decide how much of a long-term reserve fund to hold in shares. Measure 150 years of US stock market returns, find out what the bad periods looked like, estimate how risk falls with time, and set an equity share that fits the fund's horizon.

ranaroussi/quantstats ★ about 7.7kApache-2.0 licencePython

QuantStats is a widely used Python library for portfolio analytics. This lab computes its core metrics (CAGR, volatility, Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown) in the browser, and they match QuantStats to four decimals. The data is Robert Shiller's monthly S&P 500 series from the datasets/s-and-p-500 repository (public domain).

About the repository

The business question

DecisionWhat share of a reserve fund to invest in a broad stock index, given when the money will be needed.
Risk that mattersLosing purchasing power: ending the period with less, after inflation, than at the start.
MeasuresAnnual growth (CAGR), volatility, Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, and the share of past periods that ended in a real loss.

Prescriptive · what should we do?

Set the equity share

Mix shares with a simple safe asset that earns the 10-year government bond yield with no price changes. For each mix, count how often a past window of the fund's horizon ended below inflation. The committee sets the horizon and the largest chance of a real loss it accepts.
