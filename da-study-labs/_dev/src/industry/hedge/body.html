Freight Hedging Strategy Lab

An exporter that sells at fixed prices pays ocean freight every month, so every rise in freight comes straight out of its margin. Measure how much freight costs move, model the risk for next year, and test how much of next year's freight to lock in with fixed-rate contracts or a price cap.

bashtage/arch ★ about 1.6kpermissive open-source licencePython

arch is the standard Python library for volatility models such as GARCH. This lab uses it to model how freight cost volatility clusters and to forecast next year's risk. The data is the US Bureau of Labor Statistics producer price index for deep sea freight transportation, monthly since June 1988, from FRED.

Open the repository

The business question

Exposure50 forty-foot containers a month, paid at the market rate. To keep the numbers readable, one index point is taken as 10 US dollars per container.
BudgetSet each December for the next calendar year at the December rate. An overrun is the year's actual freight cost above that budget.
Hedging toolsA fixed-rate annual contract (or a freight swap) locks the December rate plus a premium. A cap pays the market rate but never more than a ceiling, for an upfront fee.
DecisionWhat share of next year's volume to lock, and whether a cap is better value.

A GARCH volatility forecast

A GARCH(1,1) model, fitted with arch, lets volatility rise after large moves and fade in calm periods. Its forecast for the next 12 months turns into a budget-at-risk figure: the rise in next year's freight that is exceeded only one year in twenty if changes are roughly normal.

Prescriptive · how much should we hedge?

Backtest the strategies, then pick one

Each strategy is replayed on every calendar year from 1989 to 2025: the budget is set in December, then the year's actual prices are paid.
